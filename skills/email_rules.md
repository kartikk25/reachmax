# Reach Max personal email rules

Write one short, plain-text note from one person to one person. Open with a specific pain point supported by confirmed facts about that business. Never invent an observation. Explain in one or two modest sentences how you can help, then end with one easy question.

Use the prospect's first name, or the existing team greeting for a general inbox. Use "I" and the sender's real first name. Avoid a corporate signature, marketing language, feature lists, buzzwords, fake familiarity, fake reply subjects, and claims that are not in the supplied facts. Keep the subject short, truthful, and lowercase-friendly. Use no more than one link, and no link in the first email unless it is needed. Use plain text only: no HTML, logo, image, tracking link, or pixel.

Email 1 should be 60 to 110 words and must stay under the send gate's 220-word limit. Follow-ups should be shorter and add a useful new detail. The system appends the required opt-out line and physical postal address as two compact plain-text footer lines; do not remove or rewrite them.
